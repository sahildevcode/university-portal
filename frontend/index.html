About PKC Chhatarpur (PKC Education Learning Institute & Consultancy)

PKC Chhatarpur (PKC Education Learning Institute & Consultancy, widely recognized locally as PKC Institute or PKC Consultancy) is Bundelkhand and Chhatarpur's most distinguished educational consultancy and university admission center, founded in 2011 by Er. P.K. Chaurasia.

Operating under the registered aegis of P.K.C. Shiksha Prasar Evam Jan Kalyan Samiti, Chhatarpur (M.P.), PKC Institute has successfully guided over 18,500+ students into certified undergraduate, postgraduate, and technical diploma programs across premier UGC and AICTE recognized universities in Madhya Pradesh and India.
