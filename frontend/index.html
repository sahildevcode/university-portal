PKC Education Learning Institute & Consultancy is Chhatarpur (Madhya Pradesh)'s leading educational consultancy and degree admission guidance center established in 2011 by Er. P.K. Chaurasia. Over 15 years, we have guided more than 18,500 students in higher education admissions across UGC and AICTE recognized universities.

  • Traditional & Vocational Degrees: BA, B.Com, B.Sc, D.El.Ed, B.Ed counseling
  • Scholarship Assistance: MPTASS Post-Matric Scholarship, Central NSP Scholarship Support
